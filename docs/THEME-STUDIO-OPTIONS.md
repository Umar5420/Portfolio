# Theme Studio options

The Theme Studio now exposes three independent preference groups:

- **Colour mood:** the original Auto, Light, Dark, Morning, Evening, Ocean, Rose, Violet and Aurora options remain available.
- **Surface style:** Classic, Glassy, Soft, Neon and the new Futuristic style.
- **Visual direction:** Cinematic luxury, Futuristic, Minimal luxury and Hybrid.
- **Animation intensity:** Balanced, Immersive and Subtle.

Visual direction and animation intensity are saved locally on the visitor's device, independently of the colour mood and surface style. The defaults are Futuristic and Immersive. The reset action returns to the original Light + Classic mood/surface defaults and the new Futuristic + Immersive experience defaults.

Reduced-motion system preferences are respected. Choosing Subtle also reduces the intensity of decorative motion.
